# ContactZen — Direction

> The independent reachability layer for go-to-market data.

## Thesis (one paragraph)

Bad contact data is the most expensive unsolved problem in revenue — and it is getting worse, not better, as AI scales outbound. The companies that sell the data cannot credibly grade it. ContactZen is the neutral arbiter that measures whether contacts can actually be reached, across any provider, and puts a dollar figure on the revenue leaking when they can't.

## The wedge

Not "intelligence" (ZoomInfo, Apollo, Clay own that). Not "verification" (commoditized). **Reachability — and the measurable dollars lost without it.**

## Why now

ZoomInfo rebranded to "Go-To-Market Intelligence Platform" and changed its ticker to GTM in 2026 — publicly betting that a strong data foundation is the prerequisite for AI. Simultaneously: +1.5% YoY Q1 growth, ~90% NRR, ~20% workforce cut, explicit shift upmarket. The incumbent validated the problem and vacated the segment. The window is open.

## The unfair advantage — neutrality

Every data vendor that could measure decay also sells the data being measured. They are conflicted referees grading their own homework. An independent grader scoring *any* provider's data on real-world reachability occupies a position none of them can structurally take.

## The product — a pipeline, not a feature

```
Contacts in → reachability analysis layered on commodity verification → gap quantified in dollars → one-page report
```

Three phases:

1. **Hands-on audit** *(current)* — almost no software. Founder-delivered. $2–5K one-time.
2. **Productized audit** *(next 6–12 months)* — automated pipeline once motion proves out. Quarterly recurring.
3. **Continuous monitoring** *(long-term)* — ongoing reachability + vendor scorecards.

The deliverable is a single undeniable number: the revenue leaking through contacts the client cannot reach. Methodology locked in [METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md).

## The moat — earned from data

Every audit records which "valid" contacts actually bounced or went dark. Over time this becomes proprietary, cross-provider reachability ground-truth no single vendor can assemble. They see only their own data and they optimize for "valid," not "did a human reply." **The wedge mines the dataset that becomes the moat.**

## The model

Lean, capital-efficient, founder-led. ~80 right-fit accounts × modest per-customer price = ~$1M ARR. Plan targets a **profitable $1–3M ARR business** — durability over hypergrowth, freedom over empire.

Pricing shape (working hypothesis, not final):
- Initial audit: $2–5K one-time
- Quarterly re-audit: $1–2K/qtr
- Continuous monitoring (later): $X/mo

80 clients × $12K/yr = ~$960K ARR.

## Target buyers

- **Primary:** VP Sales / CRO at mid-market revenue orgs. Feels wasted rep capacity personally.
- **Secondary:** RevOps leader who already suspects vendor data is rotting and needs ammo.
- **Anti-target:** Enterprise procurement with 9-month sales cycles. That is ZoomInfo's chosen ground now — let them have it.

## What this is NOT

- Not an email verification tool. Verification is the commodity layer underneath. NeverBounce / ZeroBounce / Kickbox already do it.
- Not a data enrichment vendor. The neutral position requires not selling the data being graded.
- Not a per-seat SaaS dev tool. It is a revenue tool, sold to revenue leaders, with executive-readable output.

## The closing line

*The independent referee that the data vendors can never be.*
